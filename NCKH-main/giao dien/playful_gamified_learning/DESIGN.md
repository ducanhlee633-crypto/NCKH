## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an energetic, intrinsically rewarding, and friendly educational environment bridging the youthful energy of gamified platforms with the structured utility required for academic focus. It blends three foundational aesthetics:
- **Gamified Tactility (Duolingo/Kahoot):** Chunky 3D surfaces, responsive physical click-down states (`border-b-4`), rewarding micro-interactions, streak badges, and vivid motivational signals.
- **Systematic Structure (Notion):** Clean column hierarchies, digestible card sections, clear list workflows, and functional information density that college students and educators can rely on.
- **Expressive Warmth:** Softened pastel canvas tones, high-saturation achievement accents, and oversized approachable geometry that eliminates academic intimidation.

The emotional objective is to make progress visible and delightful. Every completed lesson, flashcard review, or study streak feels tangible, satisfying, and momentum-building across all age cohorts.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-energy chromatic primaries anchored by warm, glare-free tinted surfaces. 

- **Primary Electric Blue (`#2563EB` / Active Base `#1D4ED8`):** Represents focused intellect, core navigation, standard primary calls-to-action, and platform mechanics.
- **Secondary Emerald Green (`#10B981` / Active Base `#059669`):** Reserved for victory, validation, correct responses, streak continuation, and completion states.
- **Tertiary Sun Gold (`#F59E0B` / Active Base `#D97706`):** Drives gamification currency, experience points (XP), gems, and critical streak flame indicators.
- **Accent Coral Rose (`#F43F5E` / Active Base `#E11D48`):** Designates hearts, health, urgent live-quiz timers, and deliberate destructive alerts.
- **Accent Violet (`#8B5CF6` / Active Base `#7C3AED`):** Signals master levels, boss challenges, mystery rewards, and creative sandbox modes.
- **Canvas & Containers:** Base canvas uses soft tinted pearl `#F8FAFC`, avoiding sterile pure white. Surface cards pair distinct pastel undertones (`#F0F7FF` blue tint, `#ECFDF5` mint tint, `#FEF3C7` butter tint) with subtle 2px boundary borders (`#E2E8F0` to `#CBD5E1`) to retain separation without visual clutter.
- **Text & Contrast:** Headings and actionable labels utilize Deep Ink Slate (`#1E293B`), ensuring crisp contrast ratios well above WCAG AAA benchmarks on all pastel surfaces.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the single unified typeface across display, editorial, and label tiers. Its wide aperture, rounded terminal curves, and structural geometric stability provide extreme legibility for early readers while maintaining modern clarity for older students navigating syllabus docs and flashcards.

- **Headings:** Set intentionally heavy (`800` Extrabold and `700` Bold) with snug line height and slight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to produce a chunky, friendly "game title" cadence.
- **Body:** Standardized on `500` Medium rather than light weights to maintain high contrast on pastel backgrounds and prevent visual thinning on low-resolution displays.
- **Labels & Counters:** Utilize upper weights (`700`-`800`) with generous letter-spacing (`0.02em` - `0.04em`) to establish instant recognizability in HUD (Heads-Up Display) bars, stat badges, timers, and button callouts.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a flexible grid paired with playful structured modules:
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. All interactive choice cards and mission blocks stretch full-width for accessible thumb tapping. HUD status bars (XP, Hearts, Streak) lock to a sticky top navigation rail.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Main mission roadmaps or learning modules occupy 5 columns, with contextual widgets (leaderboards, quest checklists) occupying 3 columns.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column max-width constraint (1280px) with `2.5rem` margins and `2rem` gutters. Employs a Notion-like dual-pane ergonomics: a persistent left vertical navigation spine, a centered fluid learning canvas, and a right gamification status rail.

Spacing is deliberately generous within component interiors (`space-md` to `space-lg`) to preserve a tactile, tap-friendly touch target matrix (minimum 48px height across all clickable elements).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse, moody drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **Tactile Extrusion and Crisp Boundary Lines**:

1. **3D Push-Button Extrusions:** Actionable elements use a solid, darker-toned bottom lip (`border-b-4` or `box-shadow: 0 4px 0 0 [shade]`). On click or active touch, the element physically shifts downward by `3px` (`transform: translateY(3px)`), flattening the shadow to `1px` to produce real mechanical tactility.
2. **Elevated Game Cards:** Base interactive modules feature a 2px solid perimeter outline (`#E2E8F0`) reinforced by an offset solid shadow `box-shadow: 0 6px 0 0 #E2E8F0`. 
3. **Floating HUD & Modals:** Key gamified victory modals and floating top bars use an accentuated hard outline (`2px solid #CBD5E1`) combined with a soft, punchy ambient glow (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`).
4. **Disabled & Depleted Elements:** Inactive buttons or locked quest nodes drop their 3D bottom extrusion, becoming completely flat (`border: 2px solid #E2E8F0; background: #F1F5F9; color: #94A3B8; transform: none;`), signaling total inertness.

## Shapes

The shape language centers entirely on friendly, organic, super-elliptical geometry:
- **Pill Shape (`rounded-full`):** Universally applied to badges, XP counters, streak meters, tab pills, and call-to-action buttons.
- **Oversized Curves (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` / 1.25rem - 1.75rem):** Applied to content cards, study modules, quiz selection tiles, and dialogue bubbles.
- **Strokes & Borders:** Structural containers consistently pair their radius with a crisp 2px solid border, giving elements a clear, cartoon-inspired structural finish that scales comfortably between illustration and UI.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary 3D Push Button:** Vibrant solid fill (`#2563EB`) with a darker base extrusion (`#1D4ED8`), 2px side/top border, and `rounded-full` curvature. Text is uppercase or bold title-case (`label-lg`). Press state translates down `3px` with instant tactile spring physics.
- **Success / Continue Button:** Emerald green (`#10B981`) with bottom extrusion (`#059669`). Used exclusively for correct quiz advances and reward unlocking.
- **Secondary Ghost Push:** Pure white background, `2px solid #E2E8F0`, with `#CBD5E1` bottom lip. Hover changes fill to `#F8FAFC`.

### Quiz Choice Cards & Tiles
- Multiple-choice cards are structured as chunky surface tiles (`rounded-2xl`, min-height 64px, 2px border, 4px bottom rim). 
- **States:**
  - *Default:* White background, `#E2E8F0` border/bottom lip.
  - *Selected:* Ice-blue background (`#EFF6FF`), `#2563EB` border, and `#1D4ED8` bottom rim.
  - *Correct:* Mint background (`#ECFDF5`), `#10B981` border, `#059669` bottom rim.
  - *Incorrect Shake:* Rose background (`#FFF1F2`), `#F43F5E` border, `#E11D48` bottom rim.

### Gamification HUD & Badges
- **Streak Flame Pill:** `#FEF3C7` background, `#F59E0B` border, housing an animated orange flame glyph paired with a bold numeric counter (`badge-counter`).
- **Heart / Health Monitor:** Pill container with `#F43F5E` heart icons; depleted lives appear as dimmed, grayscale silhouettes.
- **Gems / XP Tag:** Pill container with emerald or violet jewel icon and bold tracking font.

### Progress Bars
- Heavy-gauge pill track (`16px` - `20px` height) with `#E2E8F0` inset track.
- Fill uses vivid gradient or solid primary/success color with an internal horizontal gloss highlight (a lighter 2px inner stripe along the top half) and animated spring transitions.

### Quest / Notion-Style Learning Roadmap Nodes
- Vertical stepping-stone roadmap nodes composed of floating circular action buttons (`64px` x `64px`, `rounded-full`, 4px bottom lip).
- Complete nodes display an icon checkmark with a celebratory mini-star crown; current active node bounces gently via keyframe micro-animation; locked nodes remain flat slate gray with a keyhole glyph.

### Flashcard & Content Modules
- Cards combine a warm pastel header block with clean Notion-like white work areas. Internal checkboxes feature chunky 24px square boxes with `rounded-lg` geometry and satisfying physical checkmark pops on completion.
